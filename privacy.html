Privacy Policy

CircuitCurios Studio / Pinterest integration

1. Controller

The controller for data processed in connection with CircuitCurios Studio is the operator of CircuitCurios. This policy applies in particular to the Pinterest integration used by the application.

2. Purpose of the application

CircuitCurios Studio is an internal application used to prepare, manage and publish CircuitCurios-owned product and brand content. This may include images, titles, descriptions, alt text, destination links and board assignments.

3. Pinterest integration

When CircuitCurios Studio is connected to a Pinterest account, authorization is performed using Pinterest's OAuth flow and Pinterest API. Depending on the permissions granted, the application may retrieve boards and create or manage pins belonging to the authorized account.

The application requests only permissions required for the functions selected by the authorized user. Pinterest account passwords are not collected or stored by CircuitCurios Studio.

4. Data processed

Depending on the Pinterest API functions used, the application may process:

  • Pinterest account and authorization information made available through the API;
  • board information;
  • pin content such as images, titles, descriptions, alt text and destination links;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

5. Purpose of processing

Data is processed only to provide the Pinterest functionality deliberately used within CircuitCurios Studio. A Pinterest connection is established only after authorization by the Pinterest account holder.

6. Sharing with third parties

Data is transmitted to Pinterest only insofar as necessary to use the requested Pinterest functions. CircuitCurios does not sell personal data.

7. Storage and security

Where authorization tokens are stored locally, they are used only to maintain the authorized Pinterest connection. They are not made publicly accessible and are not used for unrelated purposes.

8. Revoking access

The connection between CircuitCurios Studio and Pinterest can be revoked or disconnected. Permissions may also be managed through the relevant Pinterest account settings.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CircuitCurios Studio functionality or the requirements of connected services change.

10. Contact

Questions concerning privacy and CircuitCurios Studio can be submitted through the publicly available contact channels of CircuitCurios.
